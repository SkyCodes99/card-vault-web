Delete your account

Last updated 2026-11-10

How to delete your Collect Em All account and everything saved to it - with or without the app installed. Deleting is free and you never need to explain why.

Without the app (any web browser)

  • Open the Collect Em All website (the address this page is on) and sign in with your email and password.
  • Go to Settings > Account and choose Delete my account.
  • Type your password and your username to confirm, then choose Delete my account.

In the app (phone or computer)

Settings > Account > Delete my account, then confirm the same way. The screen tells you how long the undo window is before you confirm.

Get a copy first

Settings > Account > Download all my data saves everything in one zip file: your collection as a spreadsheet (CSV), your wishlist, checklists, goals, trades, settings and your own photos. Your collection is encrypted on the server, so the file is made on your device after you sign in.

What is deleted, and when

  • Your account, your username, and every copy saved on our server: your collection, wishlist, checklists, goals, trades, settings, photos, friend profile and shared pages.
  • You can undo it for a short time (the app shows how long before you confirm) after you confirm: sign in and choose Restore my account. After that it is erased for good and cannot be brought back.
  • Server backups that may still hold the encrypted data are removed on their normal cycle (within 30 days). If a backup is ever restored, accounts that were deleted are erased again.
  • What is on your own devices stays until you remove it (Settings > Import, export & backup > Start over), because it is yours.

If you cannot sign in

Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen first. If you still cannot get in, write to thecardarena@gmail.com from the email address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. We never ask for your password.
