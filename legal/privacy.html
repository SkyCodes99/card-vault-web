Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-10

Collect Em All is a collection tracker for trading cards, sealed products and collectible toys. This page says, in plain words, what the app keeps about you, where it goes, and how to get it back or delete it.

The short version

  • Your collection is encrypted on your device before it is saved to your account. The server only holds scrambled data it cannot read.
  • Your email is used only to sign in, recover your account and send account notices. It is never shown to other people.
  • We never sell your information, show no advertising and use no third-party trackers.
  • You can download your data and delete your account yourself, from Settings.

What is kept, and why

  • Account: your username (shown to friends and on anything you choose to share), your email (for sign-in, recovery and account notices only) and a scrambled check of your password. Your password itself is never stored or sent in a readable form.
  • Your collection: cards, sealed products, portfolios, what you paid, notes and settings. Without an account it stays on your device. With an account it is also saved to your account, end-to-end encrypted: only your signed-in devices hold the key.
  • Your own photos: condition photos and pictures of items you add are shrunk and stored with your collection. [OWNER: confirm photos are saved to the account encrypted, the same as the collection]
  • Account timestamps: the day your account was created, when each signed-in device was last used and the device name you see under Devices, and when your data was last updated. The app's owner can see a count of new accounts and each account's username and joining day in an owner-only screen - never emails or collection contents.
  • Feedback you send: delivered to the app's owner with your username and the app's version - never your email.
  • Things you choose to share: friend profiles, share pages and trade offers carry only what you switch on (Friends > What friends see). A share link is public to anyone who has the link until you remove it.

What stays on your device

  • Card scanning runs on your device. Camera pictures used for scanning are not uploaded. Scan pictures are never kept, unless you choose to share them to help improve the scanner; you can change that choice at any time.
  • Your collection, if you do not sign in. Backups the app makes are saved in your own data folder or browser.

Other services the app talks to

To show card lists, pictures and prices the app downloads public information. These services see a normal web request (including your internet address) but are not sent your account or collection.

  • TCGplayer card lists, pictures and market prices, read through the community mirror tcgcsv.com, and the app's own Card Data service.
  • TCGdex and other public card databases, wikis and shop or publisher websites, for card details and pictures TCGplayer does not have.
  • eBay: the app's own price job uses eBay's official API to read the asking prices of public listings, searched by item name. Only prices are kept (with the date and how many listings were used) - no eBay seller or buyer names, IDs, messages or any other eBay member data is collected or stored. Your account is not linked to eBay and none of your information is sent to eBay. eBay prices in the app are marked "Prices from eBay" and link back to eBay. When eBay tells us that an eBay member has closed their account, there is nothing of theirs to delete; we keep only the notice's reference number and time.
  • Patreon: only if you link a Patreon account, to unlock supporter perks. [OWNER: confirm whether Patreon linking exists yet and exactly what it shares - usually your Patreon name, email and pledge level]
  • PayPal: donations open PayPal in its own window. The app never sees your payment details.
  • Currency rates (European Central Bank, through frankfurter.app) and set logos are downloaded only when you press the button.
  • Updates: the desktop app checks for new versions on GitHub, where the releases are published.
  • Hosting: your account and encrypted collection are stored on the app's account server. [OWNER: confirm the hosting provider and country, e.g. Microsoft Azure and region]

Internet addresses and logs

When you connect, the server sees your internet address. It is used briefly to stop abuse, such as too many wrong passwords, and is not saved to your account. The server does not log what you send. [OWNER: confirm what the hosting provider's own logs keep, and for how long]

What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade your information.
  • Show advertising or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Read your collection: it is encrypted before it leaves your device.
  • Show your email to anyone.

Download your data

Settings > Import, export & backup > Download all my data saves everything in one zip file: your collection as a spreadsheet (CSV), plus your wishlist, checklists, goals, trades, settings and your own photos. It is made on your device, because the server only holds your collection encrypted. Export my collection (CSV) and Backups are there too.

Delete your account

Settings > Account > Delete my account (no app? see legal/delete-account.html on this website). Your account and everything saved to it are removed from the server after a 14-day restore window, in case it was a mistake; the app shows how long before you confirm. Server backups that may still hold the encrypted data are deleted on their normal cycle (within 30 days). What is on your own devices stays until you remove it (Settings > Import, export & backup > Start over).

Keeping it safe

Your collection is encrypted with a key made from your password, on your device. This also means: if you forget your password and lose your recovery key, nobody - including us - can open the old saved collection. 2-step sign-in and email codes get you back into your account, but your old saved contents then need your recovery key or a device that is still signed in.

Children

Collect Em All is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and it will be deleted.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date, and important changes are announced in the app.

Questions or requests: use Send feedback in the app (the speech-bubble button), or write to thecardarena@gmail.com.
