Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-18

Collect Em All is a collection tracker for trading cards, sealed products and collectible toys. This page says, in plain words, what the app keeps about you, where it goes, and how to get it back or delete it.

The short version

  • Once you choose Move to instant online, your collection is stored on our server, so it opens instantly on every device. The server can read it (it is no longer end-to-end encrypted). It travels only over a secure connection and is stored encrypted on the server's disks.
  • Until you move, your saved collection stays end-to-end encrypted as before: the server only holds scrambled data it cannot read.
  • Your email is used only to sign in, recover your account and send account notices. It is never shown to other people.
  • We never sell your information, show no advertising and use no third-party trackers.
  • You can download your data and delete your account yourself, from Settings.

What is kept, and why

  • Account: your username (shown to friends and on anything you choose to share), your email (for sign-in, recovery and account notices only) and a scrambled check of your password. Your password itself is never stored or sent in a readable form.
  • Your collection: cards, sealed products, your own items, portfolios, groups and labels, wishlist, binders, trades, goals, decks, checklists, what you paid and sold for, notes, settings and the undo history. Without an account it stays on your device. With an account that has moved to instant online, it is stored on our server in a form the server can read, so it can work out values, alerts and fixes for you without an app update. Every replaced version is kept for a while so a change can be undone. With an account that has not moved yet, it is saved end-to-end encrypted: only your signed-in devices hold the key.
  • Your own photos: condition photos and pictures of items you add are shrunk and stored with your collection. After the move they are stored on our server with it (the server can see them), up to the photo limit of your plan.
  • Copies to review: when the same thing was changed on two devices, both copies are kept on a review list in your account until you choose. Nothing is thrown away.
  • Account timestamps: the day your account was created, when each signed-in device was last used and the device name you see under Devices, and when your data was last updated. The app's owner can see a count of new accounts and each account's username and joining day in an owner-only screen - never emails or collection contents.
  • Feedback you send: delivered to the app's owner with your username and the app's version - never your email.
  • Things you choose to share: friend profiles, share pages and trade offers carry only what you switch on (Friends > What friends see). A share link is public to anyone who has the link until you remove it.

What stays on your device

  • Card scanning runs on your device. Camera pictures used for scanning are not uploaded. Scan pictures are never kept, unless you choose to share them to help improve the scanner; you can change that choice at any time.
  • Your collection, if you do not sign in. Backups the app makes are saved in your own data folder or browser.

Other services the app talks to

To show card lists, pictures and prices the app downloads public information. These services see a normal web request (including your internet address) but are not sent your account or collection.

  • TCGplayer card lists, pictures and market prices, read through the community mirror tcgcsv.com, and the app's own Card Data service.
  • TCGdex and other public card databases, wikis and shop or publisher websites, for card details and pictures TCGplayer does not have.
  • eBay: the app's own price job uses eBay's official API to read the asking prices of public listings, searched by item name. Only prices are kept (with the date and how many listings were used) - no eBay seller or buyer names, IDs, messages or any other eBay member data is collected or stored. Your account is not linked to eBay and none of your information is sent to eBay. eBay prices in the app are marked "Prices from eBay" and link back to eBay. When eBay tells us that an eBay member has closed their account, there is nothing of theirs to delete; we keep only the notice's reference number and time.
  • Patreon: only if you link a Patreon account, to unlock supporter perks. [OWNER: confirm whether Patreon linking exists yet and exactly what it shares - usually your Patreon name, email and pledge level]
  • PayPal: donations open PayPal in its own window. The app never sees your payment details.
  • Currency rates (European Central Bank, through frankfurter.app) and set logos are downloaded only when you press the button.
  • Updates: the desktop app checks for new versions on GitHub, where the releases are published.
  • Hosting: your account and collection are stored on the app's account server. [OWNER: confirm the hosting provider and country, e.g. Microsoft Azure and region]

Internet addresses and logs

When you connect, the server sees your internet address. It is used briefly to stop abuse, such as too many wrong passwords, and is not saved to your account. Server logs keep only an error code, a reference, the app's version and timings - never item names, notes or your email. [OWNER: confirm what the hosting provider's own logs keep, and for how long]

What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade your information.
  • Show advertising or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Look through your collection: the server reads it only to run the app's features for you. The owner's admin screens show counts, never what you own.
  • Show your email to anyone.

Backups

The server makes a backup of every collection twice a day. Backups are kept on a tiered plan: all of the last 3 days, one a day for 14 days and one a week for up to 8 weeks, then they are deleted. Backups are stored encrypted and are used only to restore data after a fault.

Download your data

Settings > Import, export & backup > Download all my data saves everything in one zip file: your collection as a spreadsheet (CSV), plus your wishlist, checklists, goals, trades, settings and your own photos. Export my collection (CSV) and Backups are there too.

Delete your account

Settings > Account > Delete my account (no app? see legal/delete-account.html on this website). Your account and everything saved to it, including your collection and photos, are removed from the server after a 14-day restore window, in case it was a mistake; the app shows how long before you confirm. A deleted account can still be in the server's backups for up to 8 weeks, until those backups are deleted on their normal cycle; it is never restored from them. What is on your own devices stays until you remove it (Settings > Import, export & backup > Start over).

Keeping it safe

Everything travels only over a secure connection (HTTPS), and the collection, photos and backups are stored encrypted on the server's disks. The database can be reached only by the app's own server, and each request can reach only the signed-in account's data. 2-step sign-in, throttling of wrong passwords and an audit log protect the account itself.

Because the server can read collections after the move, someone who broke into the whole server could see them (items, prices paid, notes and photos), not just emails. That is the trade for instant opening everywhere; the move screen says so before you choose.

For an account that has not moved yet, the collection is encrypted with a key made from your password, on your device: if you forget your password and lose your recovery key, nobody - including us - can open the old saved collection.

Children

Collect Em All is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and it will be deleted.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date, and important changes are announced in the app.

Questions or requests: use Send feedback in the app (the speech-bubble button), or write to thecardarena@gmail.com.
